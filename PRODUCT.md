# seWer

## Register

product

## Users

Listeners using a Windows desktop music player for local files and SoundCloud libraries, playlists and search. Existing Russian and English interfaces serve the same listening workflow.

## Product Purpose

Keep playback, queues, audio effects and Discord presence dependable without interrupting listening. Settings change real behaviour, not just visual demonstrations.

## Brand Personality

Personal, compact, music-first. Follow the existing cover-driven themes rather than introducing a new visual identity.

## Anti-references

No new separators or framing in settings, explanatory paragraphs, nested cards, decorative dashboards or default glass effects. Do not change unrelated preset icons or hover behaviour.

## Design Principles

- Audio continuity comes before decoration.
- Controls should be immediately understandable and use the existing settings vocabulary.
- Extra options must deliver an audible or practical benefit.
- Preserve the user's theme and library workflow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep Russian and English labels equivalent. Use native keyboard-operable controls and accessible names. Respect reduced motion. Visual and listening verification is performed by the user; automated checks must not open the application.
